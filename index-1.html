Mental Awareness

Intermittent psychosis describes episodes where a person periodically loses touch with reality—experiencing hallucinations, delusions, or disorganized thinking—that come and go rather than persist. Triggers can include stress, sleep loss, or conditions like schizophrenia or bipolar disorder. Functioning often returns to normal between episodes. Professional evaluation and treatment help manage symptoms.
